# CampusVibe Roadmap

## Delivery Strategy

CampusVibe should be delivered in phases that prioritize operational utility first, then expand into community and media effects.

## Phase 0: Foundation

Goals:

- Establish product direction, docs, and technical conventions
- Set up shared Supabase project structure
- Define data models, auth rules, and environment strategy
- Prepare design system direction for app and web

Outputs:

- Product documentation
- Architecture documentation
- Shared backend planning
- Initial brand and UI direction

## Phase 1: Core Utility MVP

Goals:

- Launch the first useful daily workflows for students
- Validate demand around transport, food, and delivery

Scope:

- Authentication and profile setup
- University and campus selection
- Multi-role application flow
- Transport requests and driver mode
- Delivery requests and rider mode
- Restaurant ordering basics
- Notifications for active operations

Success signals:

- Initial campus adoption
- Completed transactions
- Worker onboarding and retention
- First vendor partnerships

## Phase 2: Commerce and Communication

Goals:

- Increase retention and transaction frequency
- Deepen the ecosystem loop between buyers, sellers, and service providers

Scope:

- Marketplace listings
- Direct messaging
- Wallet and payment records
- Seller tooling
- Restaurant dashboards
- Ratings and issue reporting

Success signals:

- Repeat usage across more than one module
- Growth in campus commerce volume
- Reduced reliance on off-platform chat coordination

## Phase 3: Community and Campus Network Effects

Goals:

- Expand beyond utility into daily engagement and campus identity

Scope:

- Social feed
- Club and group spaces
- Events and RSVP workflows
- University announcements
- Media content and community engagement loops

Success signals:

- Increased daily active users
- Strong event participation
- Higher content creation and sharing

## Phase 4: CampusVibe Media and Growth Engine

Goals:

- Turn the web platform into a strong acquisition and media channel
- Support sponsorship and brand partnerships

Scope:

- Editorial news publishing
- Video and podcast hub
- Awards and voting systems
- Sponsor and contributor journeys
- SEO and content distribution strategy

Success signals:

- Organic traffic growth
- Strong time-on-site and content engagement
- Partner inquiries and campaign revenue

## Phase 5: Ecosystem Expansion

Goals:

- Broaden the platform into a full university services ecosystem

Scope candidates:

- Jobs and internships
- Accommodation listings
- Tutoring and academic resources
- AI recommendations
- Loyalty and rewards
- Campus business intelligence and admin dashboards

## Delivery Principles

- Keep one shared backend source of truth
- Ship narrow, coherent workflows instead of broad but shallow features
- Build trust and verification before chasing aggressive scale
- Optimize for Tanzanian student realities including affordability, connectivity, and mobile-money-first behavior
